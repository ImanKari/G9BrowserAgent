G9 Browser Agent is installed

Three steps and your AI agent can drive this browser.

  1. Start the bridge

    From the repo root — or let your MCP client launch it for you.

    node bridge/src/server.js
  2. Point your agent at it

    Copy setup/mcp.json into your MCP client config.

  3. Attach a tab

    Open the page you want tested, click the G9 toolbar icon, then press Attach & Pin current tab. The agent is now locked to that tab and cannot wander into your other windows.

What the agent can do: read the DOM, computed styles, console, network bodies, storage and cookies; click, type, drag and scroll with real trusted input events; navigate, emulate devices, throttle the network, and capture screenshots. Everything it does is listed live in the side panel, and Stop halts it instantly.
